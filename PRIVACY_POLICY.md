# Privacy policy draft — Photo Compressor Lite

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Photo Compressor Lite processes photos locally on your Android device. It requires no account and includes no analytics, advertising, backend, internet permission, or photo uploads.

The app accesses only images you select using Android's system photo picker. Selected originals are read for compression and never overwritten. The app removes source metadata, including EXIF location, from generated images. JPEG output of transparent images requires your consent to a white background; WebP supports transparency.

Compressed outputs are kept in private application storage for saving and sharing. These files remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Up to 20 recent compression summaries (date, photo count, original and output sizes) and your theme, format and size preferences are stored locally. Clearing local history removes those summaries. Application backup is disabled.

When you save an image, a new copy is added to your device's Pictures/Photo Compressor Lite collection. Saved photos remain until you delete them through your gallery or file manager. On Android 8 and 9, write-storage permission is requested only when saving. Newer Android versions use scoped storage without broad storage permission.

If you choose Share, the receiving app receives access to the chosen compressed photos through secure content URIs. The receiving app's own privacy practices apply.

You can delete private settings, summaries and compressed files through Android's Clear storage action or by uninstalling the app. This does not delete gallery copies or your originals. We do not collect any information from children or other users.

Before publishing this draft, the publisher must add their legal name and a monitored privacy contact, and host this policy at the public URL supplied to Google Play. No developer contact was provided in the project request.
